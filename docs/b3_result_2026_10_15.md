## 6. What this result is not

- Not a new random sample: the seeds were drawn once by the rule and the prediction was fixed before the board ran; the
  primary's p is the predicted p, and a negative primary was not a possible silicon outcome of this design — a mismatch is a
  HOLD / KILL of the instrument (§1).
- Not a cross-die, cross-carrier or cross-class generalisation: one board, one carrier, the content-bit class on 292 attested
  addresses, F1, one operator, one cartographer rule.
- Not a statement about transport reliability: byte deletions on the UART path were observed in three of the ten board
  sessions of this lifecycle (B3Q -01, main 3 and 7) and corrupted lines in all of them; every session ran under a spent,
  bounded exception to the standing stop-loss, which is not lifted.
- Not a passed formal clean-tree proof: the S2 and S3 proofs failed on two pinned-test defects and were accepted for
  engineering purposes under the named exceptions (§1a); the defects are fixed only in a next lifecycle.
- Not a statement about the image's formal stack premises: `image_ready = false`, `FINDINGS` (§1b).
- Not a manifest transition: the lifecycle ends at S3 with this document; B4 (FF and routing classes, sacrificial silicon)
  needs its own image, preregistration and lifecycle.
