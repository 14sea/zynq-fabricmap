RECACK failure produces exactly three REC transmissions. The two state scenarios confirm flags of 0 and 1 after opening and closing bookkeeping, respectively.

**Verification limitations:** hostapp directly includes the current `b1_app.c`. The refused candidate, session loop, restore and TERM execute actual application code. Earlier SCORED candidates are primed through orchestrator observation and the application's `note_scored`; their actual PL staging/ARM/score paths are not executed. The two successful bookkeeping scenarios also do not represent complete successful hardware sessions. Passing RTL simulation, host models and source audits does not establish silicon qualification. The complete suite's **1425 tests / 0 skips** is the committed clean-tree record in `…T191204Z.json`. This review recomputed its artifact hashes but did not rerun the complete suite.
