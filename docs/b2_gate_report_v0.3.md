## 3. Reading

- On every fitness the self-map arm (B) beats random-safe (A), and the oracle arm (C) equals B exactly — B1's map is the certificate's mapping (292/292), so the two arms are the same operator on the same relations.
- The shuffled map (D) and the within-LUT-shuffled map (E) do **not** profit — their mean Δ is negative: a wrong map through the same move shape costs budget. The benefit is column identity, not LUT membership (G8).
- The dose–response is monotone in map quality and crosses zero between q = ½ and q = ¾: under the ½ mixture a poor map is worse than no map (`docs/b2_architecture.md` §8). This is a property of the operator, reported, not tuned away.
- Champion holdout medians are equal across arms: the search does not move holdout positions — holdout is a neutrality / known-answer control here, not a generalisation measure (§8).
- What separates the fitnesses is the **session cost** of a powered test (G5), not discrimination: the selected fitness is the first in the frozen order whose N(B*) × 2 × B* fits one two-hour session at the evidenced sampled-audit rate; whether it also fits the all-self-reporting rate is reported in G5 and is the owner's choice.
